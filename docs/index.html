Full installation guide ↗
  1. 01

    Pick your pack

    Download the individual ZIP for the ship or artwork you want.

  2. 02

    Add it to your game

    Close OpenReliant and back up the older mod. Put the new folder inside mods beside resource.hog; replace the old folder completely when upgrading.

  3. 03

    Restart and explore

    Enable the pack under Game Options → Mods, restart, and take a closer look.
