# Beta limits and planned refinements

- **Window lighting:** Refine the window lighting in a later artwork pass. The current appearance and full-range damage response are accepted for this beta.
- Requires official OpenReliant 0.18.0. Revalidate the surface shader against later engine shader changes.
- The engine surface light affects nozzle/rim surfaces; it does not illuminate nearby ships or mounted turrets. The Prowler has no separately damageable engine components. Coarse detail levels without nozzle interiors omit retained heat.
- Native Alliance turrets remain unchanged. Dynamic turret effects are not part of this release.
- Full campaign, every damage/distance transition and other platforms have not been exhaustively tested.
- The browser 3D preview has reduced textures and static lighting; use the official-engine photograph and included mission for runtime appearance.
