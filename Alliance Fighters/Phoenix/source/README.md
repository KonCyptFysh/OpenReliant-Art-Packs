# Phoenix editable source

Open `phoenix_worn_surfaces_v7.blend`. Images are packed and also use relative `resources/` paths. Prior scenes, maps and UV layers remain as authoring history.

The large intake and two upper vents have relief boundaries fitted to their painted openings, with matching fin/rail positions and neutral normals outside the openings. Main colour artwork and the corrected glazing are retained.

The central belly uses one continuous surface mapping through the forward band and aft panels. Its shared edges have matching UV coordinates. The overlapping bronze nose patches have a padded local bake with continuous Phoenix wear and aligned shallow panel cuts, using the same approach as the Naginata repair. Surrounding markings, native geometry, custom normals, attachments, damage flags, animations and lower LODs are preserved. Flight, training and loadout reuse the corrected maps.

`maps/audit_v7/` contains updated relief maps and the additional surface atlas. `recipes/audit_v7/` contains the build, export and preservation checks. Both sides, intakes, belly and nose were inspected in Blender; the user approved the installed revision 7.0 appearance for publication.
