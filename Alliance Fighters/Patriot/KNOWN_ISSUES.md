# Patriot: known work

Current compatibility target: official OpenReliant 0.8.1. See `compatibility-0.8.json` for this package-only update. All native models/missions and the PNG loadout-colour pipeline passed non-visual checks. No new 0.8.1 gameplay or GPU-rendering capture was performed. The earlier artwork and gameplay limitations below remain applicable.

The maintainer confirmed Patriot v3 finished and approved publication on 7 October 2026.

- Custom emissive design and maps remain a later update.
- The existing under-nose treatment is retained; any redesign is separate future work.
- Stock lower-detail models and later-campaign liveries remain under review.
- Full campaign play, damage, ejection, turret firing, loadout and distance transitions need broader testing.
- Windows and macOS runtime testing has not been performed.
- Optional inspection mission 992 can conflict with another mod using that number.

This beta has bounded Linux format, loading and rendering checks. It is not a full gameplay certification.
