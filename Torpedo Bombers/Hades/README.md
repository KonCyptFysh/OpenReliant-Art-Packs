# Hades - Worn Paint

**Artwork 1.0 approved for this beta.** Package **0.1.0-beta.1**, targeting official OpenReliant 0.8.1.

Restores the worn bronze-brown, green and grey Alliance torpedo bomber with US insignia, caution markings, recessed dark green glazing and restrained structural normal/roughness/metallic maps. One canonical PNG material set; the engine generates loadout colours.

The native six-part USTB_Hds model retains its geometry, UVs, normals, attachments and animations. All 881 native faces were audited; 43 strip/fan continuation counts were corrected to respect texture joins. Four sliding torpedo arms keep their original launch mounts. Quiet launchers cover stowed, deployed, cycling and timed four-torpedo release modes.

Artwork accepted by the user for this beta round. Official 0.8.1 native-format, material-loader, portable-source and installed/package checks passed. Full campaign, damage and timed-release coverage remain unverified.
