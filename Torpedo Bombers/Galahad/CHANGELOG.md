## 0.1.0-beta.1 - artwork 1.1

First approved Galahad release. Worn grey alloy, corrected silver RAF birds, inset green glazing, localized cockpit wear cleanup and corrected glass/frame material masks. Includes stowed, deployed, cycle and timed-release review modes for official OpenReliant 0.8.1, current editable Blender source, and a native-model 3D gallery preview. One unprefixed PNG material set; no duplicate loadout colour textures.
