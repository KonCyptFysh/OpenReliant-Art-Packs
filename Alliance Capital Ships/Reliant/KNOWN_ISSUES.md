# Beta scope and limits

- Requires official OpenReliant 0.18.0. Later shader/API changes may require revalidation.
- Full campaign coverage, all damage/distance transitions and other platforms have not been exhaustively tested.
- Relief is produced by normal/material maps on retained native geometry. It does not add silhouette displacement or actual service-gallery machinery meshes.
- Shared Allied_Cap_Gun.SHP and 13_al_STUR.SHP overrides also affect other ships using those models while this mod is enabled. Their material names are isolated; global Alliance weapon texture atlases are not replaced.
- Engine/cabin surface effects do not promise dynamic illumination of nearby ships. Per-barrel turret emissive animation remains outside this release.
- The 4K tier includes resampled 1254-pixel generated reconstruction sources; hangar authoring maps are 2048 pixels. Delivery resolution is not a claim of native 4K detail throughout.
- The browser model is a static reduced-texture viewing copy; engine heat, damage response, exhaust and animated sequences must be viewed in game.
