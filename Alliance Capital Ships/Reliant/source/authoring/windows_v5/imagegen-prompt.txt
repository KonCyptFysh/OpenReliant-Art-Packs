Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: high resolution game material source, a single ship observation-window insert for OpenReliant's ANS Reliant. This is texture artwork to be mapped onto an existing rectangular window, not an illustration of a spaceship.
Create one straight-on orthographic rectangular armored window unit, width exactly three times height, centered and occupying 90 percent of the width of a landscape 1536x1024 canvas. Plain uniform dark charcoal surround. Perfectly horizontal and vertical edges, no perspective, no curvature, no text.
Materials: narrow practical steel mounting bezel in worn desaturated olive-grey gunmetal, a few tiny screw heads in the outer bezel, restrained edge chips exposing dull steel, a thin black rubber gasket, glass recessed behind the frame. The glass must look like thick smoked grey-bronze laminated glazing, with subtle fine uneven scratches, tiny abrasion specks and grime collected at the edges. Faint warm off-white cabin illumination comes from behind the tinted glass, with soft indistinct internal panels and shadows, very understated silhouettes of ship interior fixtures, no people. Keep the main glass dark enough to read as glass with layered depth; illumination is muted ivory/ochre in selected patches, never a uniform bright orange rectangle.
Physical production aesthetic: utilitarian naval spacecraft, convincing scale, realistic PBR texture detail, restrained wear, no ornate decoration, no gold metal frame, no chrome shine, no glowing border, no neon, no cartoon, no external scene reflections, no exaggerated crack damage, no bloom, no cast drop shadow outside the insert. Flat neutral studio illumination for a usable texture source. Fine scratches belong to glass, chipped paint belongs to metal. The window has a single large uninterrupted rectangular pane; no central mullions.
