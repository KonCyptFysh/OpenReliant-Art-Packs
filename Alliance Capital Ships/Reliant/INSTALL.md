# Installation

Use your installed StarLancer data with official OpenReliant 0.18.0. Replace the existing `mods/119-reliant-worn-v1` folder with the folder from the chosen ZIP, enable the mod and restart. Choose one of 1K, 2K or 4K; do not merge tiers or leave old files behind. Keep pilot saves.

Enable MATERIALS, PER-PIXEL LIGHTING and MOD EFFECTS for the authored surfaces and reactive lighting. BLOOM adds the glow halo.

Player downloads contain only the asset runtime. Inspection scenes, launch/landing tests, diagnostic controls and developer launchers are kept under `dev/` and are not installed with the artwork.
