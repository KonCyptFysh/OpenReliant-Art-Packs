One actual bug I've reproduced is text corruption when the same font is drawn at more than four sizes in a frame. I've attached a small test: six sizes on the left using one font, the same six on the right using separate copies of it. The first two left-hand rows break up; the right-hand ones are fine. We're working around it with separate font instances for now. Looking at outline.zig, it seems the four-size cache can reuse an atlas while earlier text still refers to it. Could those atlases stay alive until the frame has finished drawing? Just raising the limit would probably leave the same problem further down the line.

For gunnery, could hud.guns also give us the live rounds remaining and whether the selected group is paired? We have group/charge/synchronised already, but I still need the original gunnery display on Grendel, Wolverine and Reaper to keep their ammunition visible. A nullable rounds field and a paired flag would let us handle that and the solid/split bar without guessing from the ship type.

The countermeasure count also doesn't tell us when its icon/readout should flash. Could we get those presentation values too? Something like active, visible and charge, keeping the slot reserved while the light flashes off, would let a replacement follow the native display.
